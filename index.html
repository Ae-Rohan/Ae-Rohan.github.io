01 — About Me

Engineering with simulation.

I am pursuing M.Tech in Aerospace Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, after completing B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from Walchand College of Engineering, Sangli.

My work focuses on Computational Fluid Dynamics, aeroacoustics, compressible flows, propulsion systems and turbomachinery. I enjoy using simulation to understand complex flow physics and engineering performance.

I have research and industrial experience through LPSC, ISRO and Atlas Copco, along with academic projects involving supersonic jets, variable-geometry supersonic inlets, turbofan cycle analysis and engineering design.
